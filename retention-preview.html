How kopia decides

  1. Latest N keeps the N newest snapshots, however close together they are.
  2. Every other rule keeps the newest snapshot in each period: the last one taken in each clock hour, calendar day, week (Monday to Sunday), calendar month or calendar year. Kopia counts hours, days, months and years in UTC but weeks in local time, so in summer a UK "day" runs from 01:00 to 01:00 (issue #5348).
  3. Each rule only looks back that far from the newest snapshot: daily 7 covers 7 days, weekly 4 covers 28 days, monthly 24 covers 24 months. A period that falls outside that window is ignored even if the count isn't used up.
  4. A snapshot is kept if any rule keeps it, and one snapshot often satisfies several rules (the last snapshot on a Sunday night is the newest of its hour, day and week). That's why the total is far less than the sum of the numbers.
  5. Kopia applies the rules each time it takes a snapshot and deletes everything no rule keeps. A deleted snapshot never comes back, so this list is also what you'd see in kopia snapshot list.
